# Paid bread box from the first week, no free trial

Date: 2026-10-06
Status: active
Area: pricing
Revisit: 2027-01-06 or when the reopen condition below is met

## Decision
New subscribers pay €18 from their first box. No free trial, no free first box.

## Why
We ran a free first box for 4 weeks in September. 52 people took it and 6 became paying subscribers. Each free box cost us about €7 in ingredients and a delivery slot on Saturday, which is our capacity limit.

## Discarded
- Free trial / free first box: 6 of 52 converted, and the free boxes used Saturday slots that paying customers wanted. Reopen only if: paid signups fall below 8 a month for 2 months in a row.
- 50% off the first month: same people as the free box, we expect the same low conversion. Reopen only if: we test it with a referral from an existing subscriber.

## Last check
2026-11-03: 15 paid signups in October. Reopen condition not met.
