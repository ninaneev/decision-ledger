# Bake to order, 6 pm cutoff the day before

Date: 2026-06-02
Status: active
Area: operations
Revisit: 2026-12-02

## Decision
We bake only what was ordered by 6 pm the day before.

## Why
Waste dropped from about 20% of loaves to under 3%.

## Discarded
- Walk-in café seating: no room in the shop and no staff for it. Reopen only if: we move to a bigger space.
- Daily surplus stock for walk-ins: the waste came back. Reopen only if: walk-in requests pass 30 a day.
