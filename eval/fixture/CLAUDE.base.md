# Sunday Crust

Sunday Crust is a fictional neighbourhood bakery in a mid-size European city. We bake sourdough and pastries and deliver by cargo bike within 3 km of the shop.

- Main product: a weekly bread box subscription, €18 a week, delivered on Saturday morning.
- 140 active subscribers. Team of 3. Marketing budget about €300 a month.
