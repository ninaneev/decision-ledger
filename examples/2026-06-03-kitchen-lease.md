# Sign a 3 year lease on the corner kitchen

> Fictional example. Little Oven is a made up neighbourhood bakery that delivers bread and pastries by bike.

Date: 2026-06-03
Status: active
Area: operations
Reversible: no
Revisit: 2027-06-03

## Decision
Lease the empty kitchen on the corner of our street for 3 years and move the oven there in July.

## Why
Our home kitchen cannot pass the food safety inspection we need for the bread box. The corner kitchen is 200 metres from most of our customers, so the bike routes stay short.

## Discarded
- Shared commercial kitchen across town: night slots only, and a 25 minute ride to our customers. Reopen only if: the corner lease falls through.
- Stay in the home kitchen: fails inspection. Reopen only if: never, unless the rules change.

## Supersedes
(none)

Breaking the lease early costs 6 months of rent, which is why it is marked not reversible.
