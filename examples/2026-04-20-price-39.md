# Raise the price to €39 a month, add a yearly plan

Date: 2026-04-20
Status: active
Area: pricing
Revisit: 2026-07-20

## Decision
€39 a month, or €390 a year. Existing customers keep €29.

## Why
40 customers, zero churn in 7 weeks, and nobody pushed back on price in any call. Two customers asked for an annual invoice.

## Discarded
- Stay at €29: no price objection in 40 sales, so we were leaving money behind. Reopen only if: conversion from trial drops below 20%.
- Per seat: still no team has asked. Reopen only if: a team of 5 or more asks for it.
