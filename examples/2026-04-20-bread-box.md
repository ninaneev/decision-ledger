# Sell a weekly bread box at €18, delivery included

> Fictional example. Little Oven is a made up neighbourhood bakery that delivers bread and pastries by bike.

Date: 2026-04-20
Status: active
Area: offer
Reversible: yes
Revisit: 2026-07-20

## Decision
A weekly box of two loaves and four pastries for €18, delivered every Saturday morning. Single orders stay at €3 delivery.

## Why
Most orders were the same three items every week. A fixed Saturday route lets one rider do 30 drops in two hours, and we bake to a known number with almost no waste.

## Discarded
- Keep only single orders: riders crossed the same street five times a day. Reopen only if: box subscribers drop below 20.
- Daily box: we cannot bake that much before 7am with one oven. Reopen only if: we get a second oven.
