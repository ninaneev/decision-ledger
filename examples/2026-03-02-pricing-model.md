# Charge a flat €29 a month, no free tier

Date: 2026-03-02
Status: superseded
Area: pricing
Revisit: 2026-06-01 or when we pass 50 customers

## Decision
One plan, €29 a month, cancel any time. No free tier.

## Why
Our buyers are solo coaches. In 6 sales calls, 4 asked "how much is it for just me". Per seat pricing made them do maths before they saw the product.

## Discarded
- Per seat pricing: scared off solo buyers in calls. Reopen only if: a team of 5 or more asks for it.
- Free tier: support load with no revenue, and free users in our last product never upgraded. Reopen only if: paid signups stall below 5 a month for 2 months.
- Lifetime deal: cash now, support forever. Reopen only if: we need runway in under 30 days.
