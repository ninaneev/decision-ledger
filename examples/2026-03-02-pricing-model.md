# Charge a €3 delivery fee per order, no free delivery

> Fictional example. Little Oven is a made up neighbourhood bakery that delivers bread and pastries by bike.

Date: 2026-03-02
Status: superseded
Area: pricing
Reversible: yes
Revisit: 2026-06-01 or when we pass 100 orders a week

## Decision
Every order pays a flat €3 for delivery. No free delivery threshold.

## Why
Each bike run costs us about €2.60 in the rider's time. Neighbours told us at the counter that €3 felt fair for warm bread at the door.

## Discarded
- Free delivery over €25: almost nobody orders €25 of bread, so it would only reward the rare big order. Reopen only if: average order passes €20.
- Free delivery for everyone: we lose money on every small order. Reopen only if: a rider costs us under €1 per run.
- Loyalty card: one more thing to print and track for a team of three. Reopen only if: we have more than 200 repeat customers.
